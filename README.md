# Foodsby Label Generator

## What is This?

A simple, fast tool to convert your **Foodsby PDF order files into print-ready labels**. Upload your PDFs, get a beautifully formatted A4 PDF with labels arranged in a 5×2 grid. Perfect for printing and organizing orders!

## Features

✓ **Drag & Drop Upload** - Easily upload one or multiple PDFs  
✓ **Auto-Detection** - Automatically finds and extracts labels by Order #  
✓ **Smart Cropping** - Removes whitespace, makes labels crisp and clean  
✓ **Deduplication** - Removes duplicate orders automatically  
✓ **Print-Ready** - Perfect A4 PDF (5 labels per row, 2 columns)  
✓ **Fast** - Processes instantly in the cloud  
✓ **Free** - No limits, no signup required  

## How to Use

### 1. **Visit the App**
Open your browser and go to the web interface.

### 2. **Upload PDFs**
- Click the upload area or drag & drop your Foodsby PDF files
- Upload as many as you need at once

### 3. **Generate**
- Click the **"Generate PDF"** button
- Wait a few seconds while the app processes your labels

### 4. **Download**
- Your print-ready PDF will automatically download
- Open it and print directly on your label printer!

## Print Settings

- **Format**: A4 (210mm × 297mm)
- **Orientation**: Portrait
- **Margins**: 5-15mm all sides
- **Layout**: 5 rows × 2 columns = 10 labels per page
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI (professional quality)

## What You Need

- **Browser**: Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- **PDFs**: Your Foodsby order PDF files
- **Printer**: Label printer or regular printer with label sheets

## Tips & Tricks

💡 **Batch Upload**: Upload 20+ PDFs at once - the generator handles it all  
💡 **Multiple Pages**: If PDFs have multiple pages, each page is treated as one label  
💡 **High Quality**: 300 DPI ensures crisp, professional-looking labels  
💡 **No Duplication**: The app automatically removes duplicate Order #s  

## Troubleshooting

**Q: My PDF isn't uploading**
- A: Make sure it's a valid PDF file and not corrupted

**Q: Labels look too small**
- A: Check your printer settings - ensure you're not scaling the PDF down

**Q: I see blank pages**
- A: Make sure your PDFs contain Foodsby labels with "Order #" text

**Q: Can I adjust spacing?**
- A: The layout is optimized for printing. Contact support for custom configurations.
